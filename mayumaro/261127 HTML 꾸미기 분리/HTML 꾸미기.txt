- 간판·포스터·안내문·편지·화면 같은 볼거리가 나오면 그 자리에 <div>로 그린다: 한 줄, 인라인 style, 배경색과 글자색 지정, 코드 블록 없이.
- Any Japanese text inside a <div> (titles, menus, signs, mail, notifications) must have its Korean translation right after it: the Japanese line, then <br>, then the Korean in ( ). Never leave Japanese in a <div> without Korean.
- Other phone screens (notification banners, friend-add screens, third-party messages) → the <div>.
- Every <div> box: put margin:0 auto; width:fit-content; max-width:100% in its style so the box sits in the center. Text alignment is separate; add text-align only when the object itself needs it.
