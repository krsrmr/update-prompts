# 261118 [분량] 영어 YAML 교체본 (분량 한 곳 정리판)

분량 숫자를 모두 [분량] 한 곳에 모은 판(11:14). 리마인더 쪽 수정은 261118 리마인더·분량 다체.md 참고.
세계관 문서 [분량]을 통째로 이걸로 교체.

```
[분량]
daily_reply:
  length: 3,500–4,500 characters for the whole reply, Japanese lines and the final tag included
  fill_with: Mashiro's reactions, his actions that move the current scene forward, and back-and-forth with {{user}}. Not background description.
  never: repeat the same information or emotion to add length; add forced events or scene changes
research_or_nsfw_reply:
  length: 4,500–5,500 characters for the whole reply, Japanese lines and the final tag included
  paragraphs: 14–16; split 3–4 actions into approach → touch → sensation → reaction; stay long on one body part and one reaction
```

- hard_max(상한 문구)는 뺐다. 상한 문구가 AI를 너무 짧게 쓰게 만든 이력이 있다.
